# PROCUREX Interactive Demo Center

## Overview
The Demo Center allows SIH evaluators to execute any of the 23 synthetic demonstration scenarios (A - W) and observe the complete 13-stage intelligence pipeline.

## 13 Pipeline Stages Demonstrated
1. **INGESTION** — Secure upload & magic byte validation
2. **EXTRACTION** — Structured AI field extraction & mandatory provenance
3. **REQUIREMENTS** — 17 tender requirement types normalized
4. **EVIDENCE** — Requirement vs extracted evidence mapping
5. **VERIFICATION** — 10 Government API adapters (GST, MCA, PAN, Udyam, etc.)
6. **CONSISTENCY** — Cross-document financial & identifier contradiction detection
7. **GRAPH** — NetworkX entity graph construction (shared addresses, directors, bank accounts)
8. **ANOMALY** — IsolationForest unsupervised behavioural anomaly scoring
9. **RISK** — 5-component weighted risk calculation
10. **POLICY** — Evidence-grounded Policy RAG retrieval & citations (GFR 2017 & GeM Manual)
11. **DECISION_READINESS** — Procurement officer readiness calculation (READY, MANUAL, BLOCKED, INCOMPLETE)
12. **OFFICER_DECISION** — Officer review, mandatory justification, & override recording
13. **AUDIT** — SHA-256 tamper-evident hash chain verification
