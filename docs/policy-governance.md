# Policy Governance Rules

## Core Rule
The **Deterministic Compliance Engine** remains authoritative for all numerical, threshold, and eligibility calculations.

## Policy Copilot Role
The Policy Copilot provides evidence-grounded explanations and policy context. It **CANNOT**:
- Declare a bidder compliant or non-compliant autonomously
- Override deterministic compliance calculations
- Invent policy sections or citations
- Supersede official government rules
